# Research Request: A0-06 - Telling Time and Dates

## Competency

**Name:** Telling Time and Dates  
**CEFR Level:** A0  
**Lesson ID:** A0-06

## Competency Description

The learner can:
- Tell time and understand clock times
- Name days of the week
- Name months and seasons
- Ask "What time is it?" and understand the answer
- Understand and use time expressions (morning, afternoon, evening, night)
- Understand and express dates
- Use basic time prepositions (at, on, in)
- Understand recurring time (daily, weekly, monthly)

Identify and explain the grammar concepts needed for this competency:

- **Telling time (het uur / de tijd)** -- how to express time in Dutch (hoe laat is het?), hour and minutes, o'clock expressions (3 uur, kwart over, half, kwart voor)
- **Days of the week** -- days as nouns (de maandag, de dinsdag, etc.), pronunciation, capitalization rules in Dutch (lowercase in running text, but uppercase in headings/lists)

**Critical Clarifications for Vocabulary Research:**

**A. Days and Months:**
- Days of the week should include both the standalone form and how they appear in sentences
- Note Dutch capitalization conventions (lowercase in running text: "maandag is mijn rustig dag" but "Maandag is mijn rustige dag" at start of sentence)
- Months follow similar capitalization rules

**B. Time-Telling:**
- Dutch uses 24-hour time informally in speech, but also 12-hour with "ochtend/middag/avond" context
- Key expressions: heel (o'clock), kwart over (quarter past), half (half past), kwart voor (quarter to), tegen, voorbij, over, etc.
- Pronunciation of numbers matters (times use cardinal numbers: "drie uur" = 3 o'clock, "half vier" = 3:30)

**C. Seasons:**
- Seasons are nouns with articles: de lente (spring), de zomer (summer), de herfst (autumn), de winter (winter)
- Capitalization: lowercase in running text

### 6. Assessment Criteria

How will we know if a learner has mastered this lesson?

- Can tell time using hour, half-hour, and quarter expressions
- Can state and understand days of the week and months
- Can ask and answer "Hoe laat is het?" (What time is it?)
- Can recognize and use time prepositions (om, op, in, etc.)
- Can understand and use season and month vocabulary in context
- Can schedule and describe events by day and time
- Can write or speak a simple daily schedule with times
- Can understand simple timetables or schedules in Dutch
