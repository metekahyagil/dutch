# Research Request: A0-04 - Greeting and Basic Politeness

## Competency

**Name:** Greeting and Basic Politeness  
**CEFR Level:** A0  
**Lesson ID:** A0-04

## Competency Description

The learner can:
- Greet people appropriately for the time of day
- Ask how someone is
- Respond to greetings
- Say goodbye and farewell expressions
- Use basic politeness words (please, thank you, sorry)
- Introduce themselves and others politely
- Understand and use formal vs informal greetings

## What We Need for This Lesson

For the A0-04 lesson, please research and provide:

### 1. Grammar Concepts

Identify and explain the grammar concepts needed for this competency:

- **Greetings by time of day** (goedemorgen, goedemiddag, goedenavond, hallo, tot ziens, tot straks)
- **Basic politeness formulas** (alstublieft, dank je, dank u, dankjewel, dankuwel, sorry, pardon, excuseer)
- **"Hoe gaat het met je?" structure** -- how to ask how someone is and respond
- **Present tense of "zijn" in greeting responses** -- review in context: "Het gaat goed" (It goes well), "Ik ben blij" (I am happy)
- **Leave-taking expressions** -- goodbye patterns (tot ziens, tot straks, later, doei, ciao)
- **Basic imperative forms for politeness** -- "Zeg..." (Say...), "Vraag..." (Ask...), "Doe..." (Do...) to soften requests

### 6. Assessment Criteria

How will we know if a learner has mastered this lesson?

- Can conduct a 1-minute greeting exchange with a partner, including appropriate time-of-day greeting and politeness markers
- Can greet someone appropriately for different times of day (morning, afternoon, evening)
- Can ask "Hoe gaat het?" and understand common responses
- Can say goodbye using at least 2 different leave-taking expressions appropriately
- Can use basic politeness words (please, thank you, sorry) naturally in context
- Can distinguish when to use formal "u" vs informal "jij" in greetings
