A0: Foundations ​

Welcome to the A0 level of the Dutch B1 Curriculum. This foundational level covers the essential building blocks of Dutch, including pronunciation, basic greetings, and introductions.

Competencies ​

Competency 1: Understanding Sounds ​

Learn Dutch pronunciation, the alphabet, vowel sounds, and diphthongs.

  • Lesson A0-01: Understanding Dutch Sounds and Spelling

Competency 2: Introducing Yourself ​

Learn how to introduce yourself and basic personal information.

  • Lesson A0-02

Competency 3: Describing People and Family ​

Learn vocabulary and structures for describing people and family members.

  • Lesson A0-03

Competency 4: Greeting and Politeness ​

Master greetings, politeness expressions, and basic courtesies.

  • Lesson A0-04

Competency 5: Counting and Quantities ​

Learn numbers 0-100, quantities, and basic money vocabulary.

  • Lesson A0-05

Competency 6: Telling Time and Dates ​

Learn to tell time, and use days, months, and seasons.

  • Lesson A0-06

How to Use This Curriculum ​

Each lesson includes:

  • Clear learning goals
  • Grammar explanations
  • Vocabulary lists
  • Dialogues and examples
  • Pronunciation guides
  • Practice exercises

Start with Competency 1 and work your way through sequentially.
