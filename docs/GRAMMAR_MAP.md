# Grammar Map

This document maps every grammar concept in the curriculum, tracking where it's first taught, where it's reviewed, and its dependencies.

This is the authoritative source for grammar progression. Before using any grammar concept in a lesson, check this map.

**Status:** Completed Phase 2 -- all grammar mapped from COMPETENCY_MAP.md and CEFR_GUIDE.md

---

## Format

```
| Grammar Concept | CEFR Level | First Introduced In | Reinforced In | Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal Pronouns | A0 | Introducing Yourself | All A0 competencies | None |
```

---

## Key Principles

- **One concept per lesson (mostly)**: Each lesson introduces **one major grammar concept**. Additional practice reinforces it without introducing new grammar.
- **Dependencies are strict**: A concept may only appear in a lesson **after** its dependencies have been introduced or within the same lesson.
- **Review lessons introduce no new grammar**: Review lessons after every 5 lessons consolidate and practice prior material only.
- **Lesson naming convention**: A0-01, A0-02, etc. (level-lesson number); A0-R1, A0-R2 for reviews.

## A0-B1 Grammar Summary by Category

### Verb System
- **Present Tense:** zijn (A0), hebben (A0), regular (A0), irregular (A0+)
- **Perfect Tense:** hebben + hebben (A1), zijn (A1)
- **Simple Past:** regular (A2), irregular (A2)
- **Future:** zal (A2), gaan (A2)
- **Modal Verbs:** willen, kunnen, mogen, moeten (A1), advanced nuance (B1)
- **Passive Voice:** introduction (A2), advanced (B1)
- **Reflexive Verbs:** A1
- **Separable Verbs:** A1
- **Subjunctive Mood:** B1

### Noun System
- **Articles:** de/het (A0)
- **Gender:** (A0)
- **Singular/Plural:** singular (A0), plural (A1)
- **Diminutives:** A1
- **Nouns with articles always:** A0+

### Pronoun System
- **Personal:** A0
- **Possessive:** A0
- **Demonstrative:** A0
- **Reflexive:** A1
- **Object:** A2
- **Indefinite:** A2
- **Formal (u):** B1

### Adjective System
- **Basic Adjectives:** A0
- **Adjective Agreement:** A1
- **Comparative:** A1
- **Superlative:** A1
- **Adverbial Participles:** B1

### Adverb & Word Order
- **Basic Word Order (SVO):** A0
- **Word Order after Adverbs:** A1
- **Word Order Inversion:** A2
- **Word Order in Subordinate Clauses:** A2
- **Adverbial Participles:** B1

### Prepositions
- **Basic Prepositions of Place:** A0
- **Expanded Prepositions:** A1
- **Complex Prepositions:** A2

### Complex Structures
- **Yes/No & Wh-Questions:** A0
- **Relative Clauses:** A2

- **Reported Speech:** A2-B1
- **Conditional Sentences:** B1

### Register & Style
- **Formal vs Informal:** B1
- **Imperative Mood:** A1
- **Idiomatic Expressions:** B1

3. **A0-03:** Articles (de/het), Gender System, Singular Nouns

5. **A0-05:** Negation (niet/geen)

7. **A0-07:** Possessive Pronouns

11. **A0-11:** Prepositions of Place

## Verification Checklist

Before submitting a new lesson, verify:

- [ ] All grammar used is listed on this map at or before the current lesson
- [ ] All grammar has been taught before or is being introduced in this lesson
- [ ] No grammar from a later CEFR level is used
- [ ] No circular dependencies exist
- [ ] The dependency chain is logical and complete
- [ ] The lesson introduces **at most one major new grammar concept**
- [ ] Previous grammar is reviewed but not re-introduced as "new"
