## A0-01: Understanding Dutch Sounds and Spelling

**CEFR Level:** A0  
**Competency:** Understanding Dutch Sounds and Spelling  
**Lesson ID:** A0-01  
**Topics:** Alphabet letters, Short vowels, Long vowels, Diphthongs, Consonants, Stress patterns  
**Total Words:** 25  
**Status:** Complete ✅

This lesson focuses on **phonetic teaching examples** rather than everyday vocabulary. All words serve as clear pronunciation models for Dutch sounds that differ from English.

## High-Frequency Words Strategy

Vocabulary is selected based on:

1. **Frequency** -- Most common words first
2. **Usefulness** -- Essential for communication
3. **Context** -- Grouped by topic/situation
4. **Difficulty** -- Easier pronunciation/spelling first

The "Frequency Rank" in the master database reflects this, with lower numbers being more frequent.
