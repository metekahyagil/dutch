# Research Request: A0-R1 - Review Lesson 1

## Competency

**Name:** Review Lesson 1  
**CEFR Level:** A0  
**Lesson ID:** A0-R1

## Competency Description

Review Lesson 1 consolidates and reviews all learning from Lessons A0-01 through A0-06.

A0-R1 introduces **no new grammar** and **no new vocabulary**.

Instead, it:
- Consolidates understanding of phonetics, introductions, family/descriptive vocabulary, politeness, numbers, and time/dates
- Provides spaced retrieval and active recall exercises across all six lessons
- Includes confidence-building dialogues using only taught material
- Offers cumulative reading, listening, and writing tasks
- Assesses readiness for A1-01

---

## What We Need for This Lesson

For the A0-R1 lesson, please research and provide:

### 1. Review Framework

**Identify and explain:**

- A list of all major grammar concepts from A0-01 through A0-06 (phonetics, personal pronouns, present tense of zijn/hebben, articles, demonstratives, adjectives, present tense of regular verbs, politeness formulas, numbers 0-100, time prepositions, days/months/seasons/dates, frequency expressions)
- A list of all major vocabulary categories from A0-01 through A0-06 (alphabet, sounds, names, countries, languages, family, greetings, adjectives, food/drinks, numbers, time, seasons)
- One summary statement for each lesson showing what learners should be confident doing

**Example summary for A0-01:** "You can recognize and pronounce Dutch sounds, read aloud short words using Dutch phonetic rules, and understand vowel distinctions."

### 2. Consolidation Exercises

**Design 8-10 consolidated exercises that:**

- Mix concepts from different lessons (not isolated by lesson)
- Increase in difficulty (start with simple recognition, end with complex production)
- Include all four skills: listening, reading, speaking, writing
- Use only vocabulary and grammar from A0-01 through A0-06 (no new material)
- Build confidence through successful, repeated retrieval

**Exercise types to include:**

1. **Phonetics & Pronunciation Review** -- Hearing and repeating sounds from all lessons
2. **Vocabulary Sorting** -- Categorizing words across lessons (family, time, greetings, etc.)
3. **Grammar Recognition** -- Identifying articles, pronouns, verb forms, prepositions
4. **Mixed Dialogue** -- Realistic conversation using all learned concepts
5. **Listening Comprehension** -- Multi-sentence audio using vocabulary/grammar from all six lessons
6. **Structured Writing** -- Guided writing (fill-in-blank, transformation) mixing concepts
7. **Free Writing/Speaking** -- Open-ended task (describe yourself + your week in Dutch)
8. **Cumulative Quiz** -- 15-20 questions mixing all lessons
9. **Role Play** -- Practical scenario (meeting someone, scheduling, making introductions)
10. **Self-Assessment** -- Learner rates confidence with specific can-do statements

**For each exercise type:**
- What skill it targets (listening, reading, speaking, writing, or mixed)
- Approximate quantity/duration
- One example showing the format

### 3. Cumulative Dialogues

**Provide 3-4 realistic dialogues that:**

- Use only vocabulary and grammar from A0-01 through A0-06
- Mix concepts from multiple lessons (not isolated to one lesson)
- Show practical, real-world scenarios
- Demonstrate natural progression from greeting → introduction → scheduling/planning
- Include examples of formal (u) and informal (jij) registers if applicable

**Scenarios to include:**

- Meeting someone and introducing yourself (includes name, profession, where from, family brief mention, time-specific greeting)
- Scheduling an appointment or meeting (includes day, time, date, politeness formulas)
- Brief chat about daily routines or weekly schedule (includes days, times, frequency, habitual actions)

**Format:** Dutch dialogue → English translation

**Constraint:** Use only vocabulary/grammar from A0-01 through A0-06.

### 4. Cultural Integration

**Summarize:**

- Key cultural insights from A0-01 through A0-06 (Dutch pronunciation patterns, punctuality, formality levels, capitalization conventions, time conventions, seasonal/holiday awareness)
- Practical advice for English speakers transitioning to A1 (what to expect, what remains challenging)
- One motivational note for learners completing A0

### 5. Readiness Assessment

**Define what "ready for A1" means:**

- Checklist of 10-12 concrete can-do statements (e.g., "I can tell time in multiple formats," "I can introduce myself in a full sentence," "I can read a simple text using only A0 vocabulary")
- Sample assessment tasks (3-4 realistic tasks) that demonstrate A0 mastery and readiness for A1
- Guidance on what learners should focus on if not ready yet

### 6. Teacher's Notes

**Provide optional guidance:**

- Common stumbling blocks from A0-01 through A0-06 for English speakers (half-hour confusion, preposition confusion, capitalization, etc.)
- Tips for making review engaging (games, partner activities, real-world applications)
- Suggestions for reinforcement before moving to A1
