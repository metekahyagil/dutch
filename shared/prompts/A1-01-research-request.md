# Research Request: A1-01 - Basic Sentences and Word Order

## Competency

**Name:** Basic Sentences and Word Order  
**CEFR Level:** A1  
**Lesson ID:** A1-01  
**Prerequisite:** A0-R1 (A0 complete and consolidated)

## Competency Description

The learner can:
- Construct simple, grammatically correct Dutch sentences using SVO word order
- Use present tense verbs in affirmative and negative contexts
- Form yes/no questions and simple question-word questions
- Understand and apply subject-verb agreement in present tense
- Move beyond memorized A0 phrases into productive sentence construction
- Combine A0 vocabulary and grammar to create novel, meaningful utterances

## What We Need for This Lesson

For the A1-01 lesson, please research and provide:

### 1. Grammar Concepts

- **Present Tense Verb Conjugation Review:** Recap regular verb patterns (stem + -t for singular, infinitive for plural) and confirm learners can apply this to any new regular verb
- **Subject-Verb Agreement in Context:** Show how each pronoun (ik, jij, hij/zij/het, wij, jullie, zij) requires specific verb forms; illustrate common agreement mistakes
- **SVO Word Order (Subject-Verb-Object):** Explain Dutch word order as fundamentally SVO (like English), contrasting with German's verb-final subordinate clauses
- **Yes/No Questions via Verb Inversion:** Show how Dutch forms yes/no questions by moving the finite verb to the front: "Spreek jij Nederlands?" (vs. declarative "Jij spreekt Nederlands")
- **Question-Word Questions:** Introduce 4-5 essential question words (wie = who, wat = what, waar = where, hoe = how, hoeveel = how much/many) and show that they introduce the question but verb inversion still occurs after them
- **Negation with "niet" (not):** Show where "niet" appears in sentences (typically at the end, or before predicate adjectives/infinitives)

**Format for each concept:**
- Clear explanation (as if teaching an English speaker who completed A0)
- Why it matters
- 4-6 examples with Dutch sentences and English translations
- Common mistakes Dutch learners make

### 5. Exercises (Describe What Should Be Covered)

What types of exercises should this lesson include?

Suggested exercise types (choose/describe 5-8):
- **Verb Conjugation Practice:** Given a pronoun and infinitive, produce the correct present-tense form
- **Sentence Construction:** Given a subject, verb, and object/modifier, arrange them into a grammatical Dutch sentence
- **Question Formation:** Convert declarative sentences into yes/no questions (and vice versa)
- **Question-Word Matching:** Match question words to appropriate contexts (e.g., "waar" for location, "wat" for things)
- **Negation Placement:** Add "niet" to sentences in the grammatically correct position
- **Error Correction:** Identify and correct subject-verb agreement errors, word order errors, negation placement errors
- **Listening Comprehension:** Hear a question in Dutch and select the correct answer from multiple choices
- **Speaking Scenario:** Respond to spoken/written questions in Dutch, practicing natural question-answer exchanges
- **Free Writing:** Write 3-5 simple sentences about yourself or daily life, ensuring correct conjugation and word order

**For each exercise type:**
- What skill it targets
- Approximate quantity (number of practice items or duration)
- One detailed example

### 6. Assessment Criteria

How will we know if a learner has mastered this lesson?

- Can construct 5+ original simple sentences with correct SVO word order and subject-verb agreement
- Can form yes/no questions correctly and answer them
- Can form question-word questions (using où, quoi, qui, comment, combien) and answer them
- Can negate sentences grammatically using "niet" in the correct position
- Can identify subject-verb agreement errors in others' sentences and self-correct
- Sample assessment tasks: provide 2-3 concrete tasks (e.g., "Write a 5-sentence paragraph about your day, ensuring each sentence has correct verb conjugation")
