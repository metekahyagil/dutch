# Research Request: A0-05 - Counting and Basic Quantities

## Competency

**Name:** Counting and Basic Quantities  
**CEFR Level:** A0  
**Lesson ID:** A0-05

## Competency Description

The learner can:
- Count from 0 to 100
- Understand and use cardinal numbers (0, 1, 2, 3... 100)
- Ask "How many?" and respond with numbers
- Identify basic quantities (een paar, veel, weinig, niet veel)
- Understand and use basic money vocabulary (euro, cent)
- Ask prices and understand simple monetary amounts
- Use numbers in context (ages, quantities, dates)
- Understand ordinal numbers (eerste, tweede, derde, etc.) in basic contexts

## What We Need for This Lesson

### 1. Grammar Concepts

Identify and explain the grammar concepts needed for this competency:

- **Cardinal numbers 0-100** -- how to count, number agreement with nouns, pronunciation of compound numbers (21, 34, 56)
- **"Hoeveel?" (How many?) question formation** -- yes/no vs wh-question word order, response structures
- **Ordinal numbers** (eerste, tweede, derde, vierde, vijfde, zesde, zevende, achtste, negende, tiende, elfde, twaalfde... and compound ordinals 21st, etc.)
- **Plural noun forms** -- introduction to Dutch plural morphology in context of counting ("twee appels", "vijf katten", etc.)
- **Number-noun agreement** -- when to use specific number forms and how nouns change
- **Basic monetary expressions** -- "euro", "cent", "kost", "prijs", simple price statements ("Dit kost 5 euro", "Hoeveel kost het?")

### 4. Cultural Notes

- **Ordinals in Dutch life** -- how ordinals appear in everyday contexts (floors of buildings, positions in lists, rankings)

### 6. Assessment Criteria

How will we know if a learner has mastered this lesson?

- Can count aloud from 0 to 100 with correct pronunciation
- Can listen to numbers and repeat them accurately
- Can ask and answer "Hoeveel?" questions with objects or pictures
- Can understand and respond to price questions in euros and cents
- Can read and speak numbers in context (ages, quantities, ordinals)
- Can use quantity words (veel, weinig) appropriately
- Can understand ordinal sequences (first, second, third, etc.)
- Can understand simple texts or dialogues containing multiple numbers and quantities
