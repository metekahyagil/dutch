# Research Request: A0-03 - Describing People and Family

## Competency

**Name:** Describing People and Family  
**CEFR Level:** A0  
**Lesson ID:** A0-03

## Competency Description

The learner can:
- Identify family members and their relationships
- Describe people using basic adjectives (tall, short, old, young, friendly, kind)
- Point out people and objects using demonstratives (this, that)
- State who belongs to whom using simple possessive structures
- Count and name family members
- Describe people in photographs or in real life using simple sentences

#### Critical Clarifications for Adjective Agreement

**A. Complexity Level:**
- Keep adjective agreement rules **simplified for A0 learners** (the core "-e ending" pattern for attributive adjectives with de/het/plural).
- **You may include a light preview of exceptions** (e.g., "In predicative position, adjectives don't take -e endings: De man is groot. But in attributive position: De grote man.").
- Do NOT go deep into inflectional nuances (e.g., no/neuter nominative/accusative distinctions). Save that for A1+.
- Aim for practical A0 pattern recognition, not linguistic exhaustiveness.

**B. Rule Structure for A0:**
The simplified rule should be something like:
- **Predicative (after "zijn"):** Adjective stays bare. "De moeder is oud."
- **Attributive (before "de" noun):** Add "-e" to adjective. "De oude moeder."
- **Attributive (before "het" noun):** Add "-e" to adjective. "Het oude huis."
- **Attributive (plural):** Add "-e" to adjective. "De oude huizen."

Note: This is simplified and will need reinforcement and refinement in later lessons, but it gives A0 learners a working rule.
