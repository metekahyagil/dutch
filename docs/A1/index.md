# A1 Level: Beginner

**CEFR Level:** A1 (Beginner)

**Total Lessons:** 11 content lessons + 1 review lesson

Welcome to the A1 (Beginner) level of the Dutch B1 Curriculum. At this level, you'll build on the foundations from A0 and develop the ability to construct simple sentences, ask and answer basic questions, describe daily routines, and express simple opinions and preferences.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of A1, you will be able to:

- **Construct simple sentences** with correct subject-verb agreement and word order
- **Ask and answer yes/no and question-word questions** in present tense
- **Describe daily routines** using regular verbs and adverbs of frequency
- **Talk about food, shopping, locations, clothing, and professions** using basic vocabulary and simple sentences
- **Express simple opinions and preferences** using basic adjectives and modal verbs
- **Use basic past tense** to talk about recent activities
- **Navigate simple social interactions** with increasing confidence and appropriate register

---

## Competencies

### Competency 1: Basic Sentences and Word Order

Master present-tense verb conjugation, SVO word order, the V2 (verb-second) rule, question formation through verb inversion, question-word questions, and negation with "niet" and "geen."

- **Lesson A1-01:** Basic Sentences and Word Order

---

## Next Steps

Begin with **Lesson A1-01: Basic Sentences and Word Order** to build your foundation in present-tense sentence construction and basic questioning.

After completing A1, you'll move to the **A2 (Elementary)** level, where you'll explore more complex past tenses, conditional statements, and expanded vocabulary for real-world contexts like housing, healthcare, and travel.
